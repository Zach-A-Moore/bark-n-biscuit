Our Kitchen & Grooming Philosophy

Crafted in Our Kitchen, Loved in Every Bowl & Bath

At Bark 'n' Biscuit, we believe dogs are family members who deserve wholesome food and gentle, loving grooming care. All the bakery recipes we bake were created right in our own home kitchen using 100% natural ingredients—the exact same clean, wholesome items you would find at your local grocery store.

We are deeply committed to our local community. All of our ingredients are sourced locally, so whenever you buy a treat or book a grooming visit with us, you are supporting at least 8 other local small businesses right here in our area!

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our bakery ingredients, grooming policies, and store visits.

All of our treat recipes are created in our own kitchen using 100% natural, wholesome ingredients you'd find at your local grocery store (like rolled oats, organic pumpkin, natural peanut butter, local honey, and free-range eggs). We never use artificial preservatives, dyes, or fillers!
We source all of our raw ingredients locally—from oat millers and egg producers to honey farms and meat suppliers. Every single batch of treats or grooming service you purchase directly supports at least 8 other local small businesses in our community!
You can visit our retail counter inside Brewhalla (1702 1st Ave N) to pick up fresh baked biscuits and chews anytime! For custom birthday cakes or pupcake party orders, send us a message through our inquiry form or email us at barknbiscuitsbakery@gmail.com!
To ensure the health and safety of all our furry guests, we require up-to-date Rabies, Distemper (DHPP), and Bordetella vaccinations prior to your dog's grooming appointment.
Grooming prices are based on your pet's weight, coat type, length, and coat condition (e.g. matting or heavy undercoat). You can use our interactive Grooming Price Calculator above for a fast estimate!
